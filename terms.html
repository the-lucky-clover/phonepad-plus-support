App license

For PhoneTrackPad obtained through Apple’s App Store, use of the app is subject to Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement and any applicable Apple Media Services terms, unless a different license is provided with the app.

Compatibility and responsible use

PhoneTrackPad sends input to a computer you choose. Use it only on computers you have permission to control and verify the computer’s identity when pairing. Bluetooth compatibility may vary by device and operating system. Features and compatibility may vary with device, operating system, permissions, and Bluetooth conditions.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple under its purchase and refund policies. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem to review an eligible purchase or request help with a refund.
