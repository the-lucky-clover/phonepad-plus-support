Updated November 19, 2026

These notes explain where to find the license and help for Phonehenge.

App license

For Phonehenge obtained through Apple’s App Store, use of the app is subject to Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement and any applicable Apple Media Services terms, unless a different license is provided with the app.

Compatibility and responsible use

Phonehenge sends input to a computer you choose. Use it only on computers you have permission to control and verify the computer’s identity when pairing. Direct Bluetooth is experimental and may not work with every device; Wi-Fi requires the separately run computer receiver. Features and compatibility may vary with device, operating system, permissions, and network conditions.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases made through the App Store are handled by Apple under its purchase and refund policies. Visit Apple’s Report a Problem to review an eligible purchase or request help with a refund.

Privacy and support

Read the privacy policy to learn how Phonehenge handles information. For support, email theluckyclover@gmail.com.
