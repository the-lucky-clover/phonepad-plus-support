Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026

PhoneTrackPad is designed to send your input from your iPhone to a computer you choose. This policy explains the information the app uses and the choices available to you.

Information used to provide PhoneTrackPad

When you use the trackpad or keyboard, PhoneTrackPad sends pointer movements, clicks, scrolling, key presses, and text you type to the computer you select. The application on that computer receiving the input may process, store, or transmit it under its own practices. Avoid entering information you do not intend to send to that computer.

PhoneTrackPad stores whether you have completed or skipped the introductory walkthrough in the iPhone app’s local preferences. Connection details, including pairing information, are used for the active session. Your iPhone and computer operating systems may separately retain Bluetooth pairing records and device caches.

Bluetooth connections

In direct Bluetooth mode, PhoneTrackPad transmits keyboard and mouse reports to a connected computer and uses a device identifier to manage the session. A paired computer can request the iPhone’s battery level. PhoneTrackPad asks you to approve a connecting device before sending input. Bluetooth pairing and bond management are handled by the operating systems. The app cannot independently verify the identity of the computer; approve only a connection you initiated.

Services and disclosures

The PhoneTrackPad app does not include an advertising or analytics SDK and is not designed to send your input to a developer-operated server. The computer you connect to, the operating systems involved, and any application receiving your input have their own data handling. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may receive technical information such as IP addresses and request details when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

If you contact us by email, your message and address are used to respond and handle your request. Email providers process those communications under their own policies. If you open a public GitHub issue, its contents are public; do not include passwords, pairing codes, private keys, or other sensitive information.

Retention and your choices

  • On the iPhone, you can remove PhoneTrackPad’s local preferences by deleting the app. Your operating system may retain Bluetooth bonds separately; remove those in the Bluetooth settings on each device if desired.
  • You may request access to or deletion of information you sent us by contacting the address below. We will review requests in light of applicable law and any records we need to retain.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, email theluckyclover@gmail.com. We may update this policy as PhoneTrackPad changes; the effective date above identifies the current version.
