Trackpad

Move and click

Move one finger to control the pointer, tap to click, use two fingers to scroll, and press and hold to drag.

Keyboard

Type from your phone

Open the keyboard in the app when you need to type. A US keyboard layout is currently required on the computer; emoji and accented characters aren’t supported.
